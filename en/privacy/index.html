Privacy policy

Color Dice works without registration and does not send roll results to a server. This policy covers local game settings, Google Analytics, and data that may be processed by advertising services.

Local game settings

Color Dice stores the selected theme, sound setting, roll count, and latest result in localStorage. The browser uses these values to restore the game on the same device. The site does not use them to collect a name, address, precise location, or payment information.

Analytics

Google Analytics measures visits and interactions with the site and may use analytics cookies or similar technologies. Roll results and local game settings are not sent to Google Analytics.

Advertising and cookies

Since August 18, 2026, the Russian and English homepages have used four Adsterra formats: a 300×250 banner, Social Bar, Popunder, and a Smartlink advertising link. The banner and link appear inside a labeled advertising rail beside the game card, or immediately after it on mobile screens. The advertising rail is not part of the game buttons or result area. Social Bar may display an advertising widget over the edge of the page. Popunder may open an advertising page in a separate tab or window after a site interaction. Smartlink opens only when the labeled sponsored link is selected and does not replace the current game page.

Adsterra and its advertising partners may use cookies, pixel tags, IP addresses, and device or browser information to deliver ads, control frequency, and measure performance. Details are available in the Adsterra Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy.

If Google AdSense ads are enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this site or other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to choose ads based on those visits.

Google and its advertising partners may also place and read cookies or use web beacons, IP addresses, and other identifiers. These technologies support ad delivery, frequency control, measurement, and personalization according to the user's choices. Google explains this processing on its Business Data Responsibility page. You can opt out of personalized Google advertising in Google Ads Settings. To opt out of personalization from participating vendors, visit www.aboutads.info/choices.

Advertising consent

Before personalized ads are enabled for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the site must use a Google-certified consent management platform. Its message will let visitors consent, refuse, or manage their choices. Personalized ads will not be requested without the required consent signal. Depending on the visitor's choice, Google may serve non-personalized or limited ads where supported.
